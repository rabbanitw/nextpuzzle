Trust / Accessibility

Accessibility statement.

Next Puzzle aims to make its website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

Reviewed September 7, 2026

Our commitment

What we support

We design for keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful structure, readable contrast, responsive text and layouts, form labels and status messages, reduced-motion preferences, and descriptive page titles.

Known limitations

The interactive service cube is decorative as well as functional. Its service content remains available through keyboard-accessible controls and text panels. Some linked third-party services or documents may have accessibility characteristics outside our control.

Feedback and accommodation

If you encounter a barrier or need content in another format, use the website inquiry form and select “Other.” Describe the page, task, and assistance needed. We will review the request and work toward a practical response.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing practice. We review the site as content and functionality change and prioritize fixes based on user impact.
